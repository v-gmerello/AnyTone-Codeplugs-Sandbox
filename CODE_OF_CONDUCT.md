# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to a professional, inclusive, and harassment-free community.
Participants should be able to contribute regardless of experience, identity,
background, disability, or personal characteristics.

## Expected Behavior

- Communicate respectfully and focus criticism on ideas and code.
- Welcome questions and provide actionable technical feedback.
- Acknowledge uncertainty, correct mistakes, and respect project safety rules.
- Protect private radio, identity, and security information.
- Accept maintainer decisions intended to protect contributors or users.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or unwanted attention.
- Deliberate disruption, intimidation, or sustained off-topic conflict.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Encouraging unsafe, unlawful, or unauthorized radio operation.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently
restrict participation when behavior is inappropriate, unsafe, or harmful.
Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately, and made
without retaliation.
